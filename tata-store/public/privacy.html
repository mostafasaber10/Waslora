Privacy policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy describes the information used by this self-hosted Tata Store service. The operator should update it with their legal identity and contact details before opening the store to the public.

Information developers provide

When you create a developer account, the store saves your email address and a salted password hash. It does not save your plain-text password. App submissions include the name, version, description, category, icon URL, and uploaded Android package you provide.

Sessions and security

After sign-in, the store sets a first-party, HTTP-only, same-site session cookie. Production mode marks the cookie as secure so it is sent only over HTTPS. Sessions live in memory and are cleared when the service restarts or expire after seven days.

Download counts

The store records the total number of package download requests and their calendar day for each app. The rate limiter temporarily keeps source network addresses in memory for up to one hour; it does not add them to download analytics or account records. Download analytics do not intentionally store a visitor identifier or app installation receipt. A request can represent a resumed transfer and does not prove an installation.

Storage and sharing

Developer account details and catalog records are stored on the server's persistent data volume. Published app information and APK packages are public and can be downloaded by anyone. The server operator's hosting provider may process connection data under its own policies.

Retention and deletion

Developers can remove an app and its package from their dashboard, or delete their account to remove their account, app listings, and packages. Copies retained by server backups or infrastructure logs may persist according to the operator's backup and hosting settings.

Third-party services

The store does not include advertising trackers or third-party analytics scripts. The Waslora download link redirects to GitHub, which handles the actual APK transfer under GitHub's own privacy policy.

Contact

Before public launch, the store operator should replace this section with a monitored privacy contact address and legal business details.
